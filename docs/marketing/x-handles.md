## Regulated-brand mandatory hold

@coinbase** (regulated/public companies) require explicit operator sign-off AND
the operational-readiness gate (network wallets funded + e2e passing) before
posting. A regulated brand's social team will check whether the claim works
before amplifying — a reproducible failure is a public dunk.
