Watch payments arrive as they happen

One SSE message per verify and per settle, straight off the wire. It is a live hint and not a ledger: nothing is replayed, and operations that fail are not published.

· events 0 · settles 0 · last -

Connected and waiting. Silence is not a failure — the rail measures ~1.3 settles/min and goes half an hour at zero.

timekind networkok endpointseller payeramount tx 


  
What this page CANNOT show you: the keepalives. The facilitator sends an SSE comment every 15s to hold the connection open, but EventSource never exposes those to JavaScript. That is why the indicator above is the only thing separating «nothing is happening» from «the connection dropped» — watch it, not the empty list.

The stream is lossy on purpose: it will never slow down or fail a payment so an observer can keep up. An event that happened while you were disconnected exists nowhere. Treat it as a live hint, not as a ledger — the chain is the source of truth. And operations that fail are not published, so a rail that looks healthy here has not been shown to be healthy.
