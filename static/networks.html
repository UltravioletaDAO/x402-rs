Two things this table does not claim. The tokens column is what the facilitator will settle on that chain, not what your seller accepts — the seller's 402 decides that, and x402_accepts narrows it. The balance column is the facilitator's own native-token balance, the gas it spends on your behalf; it is not your money and it is not the payment. A dash means the balance was not reported for that network, never that it is zero.

The same chains, in CAIP-2

Every network here also answers to a CAIP-2 identifier, and both spellings work everywhere a network is named. They are not shown next to each other, and that is a deliberate refusal rather than an omission: /supported publishes the two spellings as separate entries with no field linking them, and for most chains it publishes no token list under the CAIP-2 form either — measured on 2026-09-02, 49 of the CAIP-2 entries carry no tokens. Pairing them from the page would mean guessing, and a wrong chain id printed next to the right network is worse than no chain id at all. The list below is verbatim from the endpoint.

Reading a row

The schemes column matters more than it looks. exact is the ordinary x402 payment. upto authorises a ceiling and charges what was used, and is advertised only where the Permit2 proxy is actually deployed. escrow and commerce hold funds until a condition is met. fhe-transfer is confidential and experimental. A network with only exact cannot do the others — that is not an omission, it is the answer.
